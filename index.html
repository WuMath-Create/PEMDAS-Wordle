HOW TO PLAY

Fill in the blanks with digits to find the hidden equation. You get 6 tries.

Every guess has to be true. It must actually equal the target, so remember the order of operations: multiply before you add or subtract. If a guess misses, you'll see how it works out step by step.

After each guess, the tiles change color. Say the hidden equation is 18 + 2 × 3 = 24 and you guess:

2

3

4

24
  • Green: the 1 is right, in the right spot.
  • Yellow: 2 and 3 are in the equation, but in other spots.
  • Gray: there's no 4.

⏭ skips to a new puzzle, 🏳 gives up and shows the answer, and clicking the target lets you pick your own. Every puzzle you finish counts toward your stats (giving up counts as a loss). New puzzles every midnight (Eastern).

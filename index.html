PEMDAS Wordle

HOW TO PLAY

Guess the mathematically correct equation following PEMDAS order of operations!

  • Green tiles mean the character is in the correct spot.
  • Yellow tiles mean the character is in the equation but in the wrong spot.
  • Gray tiles mean the character does not appear in the equation.
  • Every guess must be a mathematically valid equation (e.g., 3+4*2=11).
